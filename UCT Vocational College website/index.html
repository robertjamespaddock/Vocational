Questions

What students and parents ask us.

Are the qualifications recognised?

Our QCTO Occupational Qualifications are registered on the National Qualifications Framework. UCT Industry Programmes lead to a UCT Industry Certificate. It is a UCT credential rather than an NQF qualification, but the programme may include QCTO Skills Programmes that carry credits towards future qualifications.

How much does it cost?

Fees are designed to be well below a traditional campus, with no travel or residence costs. Register your interest and we’ll send current fees for your path.

Can I really get work experience studying online?

Yes. Every pathway includes real work: placements with partner companies, industry projects, or managed virtual workplaces with roles, managers and deadlines.

What do I need to study?

A laptop or smartphone and an internet connection. Learning is designed for real South African connectivity, with live sessions and clear weekly structure.

Will AI do the work for me?

No. You get personalised AI support for explanations, practice and feedback, and you learn to use AI the way professionals do. The work and the learning stay yours.
